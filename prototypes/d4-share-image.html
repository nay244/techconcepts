The share image

One 1200 × 630 card for every page of the site. A chat app or social site shows it small, beside a title and a description it prints itself, so the card carries three things: the name, the mark, and the site's own line beside the figure that lifts the lid.

A is the one to take. The porcelain figure keeps its black inside at every size. B has more weight in a feed and matches the favicon's dark tile, but its figure is charcoal on near-black and closes up when the image is small.
