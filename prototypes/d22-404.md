# D22: the 404 page in the "Card stack" look

Job 23, design unit D22 (direction 2.84, 2.116, 2.117). The 404 page (`src/pages/404.astro`, served for any unknown address) in Card stack, colour variant A, as on Home (D17), Tracks (D18), the track page (D20), About and the Glossary (D21). The owner kept the layout (2.117), so there is **one layout**: today's structure, the words beside the `notFound` figure at desktop and the figure first on a phone, restyled, and changed inside only where measured (§2).

The prototype is `prototypes/d22-404.html`: one self-contained file, built from today's `dist/404.html` (`revamp` b14f4d1; d275dd9 changes documents only): its header, footer, fonts, figure and Patch's tokens as they are, then `src/styles/card-stack.css` as it is, then one block headed "D22 404". The small dark switch at the foot's left is the prototype's own (**Light**, **Dark**); the header's own theme button and menu button work too. `d22-404.html#th=dark` opens it dark; `#bar=0` hides the switch; `__d22.set('dark')` sets the theme from a script. The links are the built page's absolute ones (`/`, `/tracks`, `/how-the-internet-works/what-is-http`), so from the file system they lead nowhere. Resize the window for the widths. The script at the file's foot is the prototype's and none of it is built.

## 3. Tokens, and the shared Card stack stylesheet

`404.astro` imports `src/styles/card-stack.css` after `site.css`, as the other five pages do. **No rule of that file changes**; its opening comment (lines 1 to 3) names a sixth importer, the 404 page (`src/pages/404.astro`). No token is added anywhere: every colour on the page is a Patch token or a `--cs-*` name, and the page's styles hold no colour value.

What the file does to this page by being imported, and the design draws it so:

- **The two buttons** (`main .btn`): 48px tall, a 2px `--cs-edge` edge, 8px corners, 18px Barlow, the 3px offset edge that closes when pressed. "Go to Home" is filled `--cs-act`; "See the tracks" is the card's paper. Hover, pressed and focus are the file's own.
- **The figure** (`#main .lm-svg`, `main { --fig-bg … }`): re-tinted, not redrawn: white faces on the card's paper, ink line, no glow, no rings, the lit link lights and the loose plug in `--cs-here`.
- **The link**: `card-stack.css` holds no link colour. The page's own `.notfound__why a { color: var(--link) }` is removed, so the link is ink with the global underline, as on the other Card stack pages.

Classes taken from the file: `cs-card`, once, on the figure's wrapper. `cs-tab`, `cs-card--beneath` and `cs-card--soon` are not used: the card has no name that is already a word on the page, nothing lies behind it, and it is not waiting. Nothing new goes into `card-stack.css`: no part of this page is worn by another page.

`site.css` stays imported: it still gives the buttons their base (`.btn`: `inline-flex`, centred, the pointer). It is in the page through the header either way.

Colour, one job each: **orange** (`--cs-act`) fills the one button that is the way out, Go to Home; **orange** (`--cs-here`) is the loose plug and the seven lit lights in the figure. Nothing else on the page is coloured.

## 11. Decisions, one line each

1. The heading stays on the ground and only the figure is a card: on all five Card stack pages the opening's words are on the ground and the things beside them are cards.
2. The figure's card is flat, with no tab: nothing lies behind it, and no word on the page is its name.
3. "Error 404" is a chip of the lesson-number family, in its place above the heading: the look's own small label, with the code still the first thing read.
4. The line over the paragraph is the quiet 1px `--cs-hair`, not the 2px ink: the paragraph is a note under the two ways out, and two ink edges already sit right above it.
5. The link is ink: `card-stack.css` has no link colour and no Card stack page uses blue.
6. Scroll padding 80px, 403px under the open menu, 12px at the foot: the same rule and numbers as About and the Glossary, since the page may not change the header.
7. One row of buttons on every phone, by giving up side padding: it puts both ways out on the first screen at 320 × 640 and takes 60px off the page.
8. The buttons grow to share the row on a phone, so their offset edge ends where the card ends.
